## Observations

1. **Cup measurement & mask defects (invalidates unverified `step5_descriptors`, `step5b_associations`, `step7_focused_pushing`)**:
   - **Fragmented targets**: Target mask is split into separate pieces (`cup_01` `10.2 um`, `cup_03` `9.2 um`, `cup_09`, `cup_10` `5.1 um`, `fit_unreach_01`, `deep_01`), so the 3D convex hull bridges the inter-piece gap. Across `4,523` contact visits, median cup depth is `1.35 um` (`sphericity < 0.5`, `n = 1,925`) vs `0.72 um` (`sphericity >= 0.8`, `n = 292`; Spearman `rho = -0.26`).
   - **FOV edge truncation**: `cup_05`, `cup_13`.
   - **Two merged targets**: `dasa_vs_untr_untreated_02`, `deep_06`, `deep_07` (`5.6 um`), `deep_08`.
   - **Hollow target shells**: `dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, 11, 14, 15`, `deep_vs_shallow_shallow_10, 11` (`5.8–6.8 um` false cups).
   - **Donor `hd10` (`v09`, `v12`)**: Mostly fragmented or hollow (`dasa_vs_untr_untreated_01, 03, 06, 07`; `dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, 11, 14, 15`) plus tiny fragments (`dasatinib_16`: `37 um^3`, `dasatinib_13`: `154 um^3`).
   - **Clean cells**: Whole, separate cells measure sensibly and match raw membrane contours (`cup_14`–`16`, `shallow_12, 13`, `deep_04`, `dasa_vs_untr_untreated_04, 05, 08`).
2. **Mechanical model defects (invalidates `step6b_fits`)**:
   - Solved target drifts `2–3 um` off the mask (`fit_reach_03, 04, 05`; `fit_unreach_10, 12`).
   - Re-solving at fitted `Pi` disagrees with the continuation curve in `9 / 32` fit panels (`fit_reach_01, 03, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 13`; `fit_unreach_09`).
   - Reference target misaligned (`fit_unreach_03, 10, 12, 13, 15`), starting overlaps up to `11.0 um` (`overlap_01, 03, 12`), and curve inversion mismatch in `fit_reach_14`.
3. **Death label defects (blocks `step4_death_labels_v2`)**:
   - Narrow signal margin (`404 ADU` threshold vs `~400 ADU` background; `death_killed_01` at `404.3 ADU`).
   - Overlap bug check needed on `death_dasatinib_30` v5 (`27 px` reported outside outline).
   - Gallery/table mismatch on `death_surviving_19`, `death_surviving_24`, and `death_killed_07`.
   - Neighbour projection overlap on `death_surviving_17`, `death_killed_05`, `death_killed_09`.

## Decision

Established the stage-gate verification plan (`status/verification_plan.md`, Gates `G0`–`G5`). Every dataset after Step 3 (`step4_death_labels_v2`, `step5_descriptors`, `step5b_associations`, `step7_focused_pushing`) is marked **UNVERIFIED**, and `step6b_fits` is **REJECTED / PARKED**. No stage may feed the next until its real-image gallery passes panel-by-panel review.
