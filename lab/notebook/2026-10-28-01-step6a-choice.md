## Question

Which 3D cell-mechanics solver architecture can take segmented T-cell and target meshes from `data/scidb_19235_masks/`, apply a local active synaptic pushing load on the T-cell contact patch, represent distinct T-cell vs target cortical stiffnesses (including `~2x` stiffer synaptic cortex per Jung et al. 2021), map explicitly to AFM cortical tension $T$, and solve fast enough (`< 1 s` per relaxation) to fit hundreds of chamber-visits?

** Verification Notice: Downstream Step 6 mechanics work is UNVERIFIED and parked at Gate `G5`.**

## What was run

Source audit of `external/SimuCell3D`, `external/ShElastic`, `external/Phase_field_Apoptosis`, and timing/feasibility benchmarks documented in `.agy_step6a_report.md` and `status/plan.json`.

## What was looked at

- C++ source files in `external/SimuCell3D/src/` (`io/mesh_reader.cpp`, `solver.cpp`, `forces/`).

## Observations

1. **`SimuCell3D` (`external/SimuCell3D`, Candidate A) rejected without C++ rewrite**:
   - Requires legacy ASCII VTK polyhedra (`CELL_TYPES 42`) with strict genus-0 manifold topology and runs Ball-Pivoting / edge-refinement that fails on concave or handle-containing masks.
   - Pressure `cell::pressure_` is uniform across the entire cell (`<INMForce>` is unimplemented in C++); face types reset to `0` at every time step (`solver.cpp:124`), preventing a `2x` stiffer synaptic patch without modifying C++ source.
   - Explicit dynamic integration requires `10^4–10^5` steps (`~37.5–375 s` per forward relaxation), which is too slow for multi-chamber parameter fitting.
2. **`ShElastic` and `Phase_field_Apoptosis` rejected**:
   - `ShElastic` assumes a single homogeneous spherical target interface; `Phase_field_Apoptosis` is a regular-grid phase-field solver that does not take segmented 3D surface meshes.
3. **Custom Python quasi-static triangulated cortical shell (`scipy` L-BFGS-B, Candidate B1) selected**:
   - Consumes `marching_cubes` + `trimesh` surface meshes directly, supports heterogeneous tensions ($T_{\text{target}}$, $T_{T,\text{back}}$, $T_{T,\text{syn}} = 2\,T_{T,\text{back}}$) and local synaptic pressure $p_{\text{act}}$, and relaxes in `~0.17–0.25 s` per solve.

## Decision

Selected the custom Python quasi-static two-shell cortical model (`src/mech/shell_model.py`) for Step 6b (`commit 159df33`). All subsequent Step 6b runs remain **UNVERIFIED** and parked at Gate `G5`.
