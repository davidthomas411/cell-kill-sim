Part I Physics Layer: Mechanical Parameters, Structural Identifiability Map, Perforin/ESCRT Repair ODE & Held-Out Dasatinib Perturbation Test

Instead of an area-only geometric proxy capped at 1.55 nN, synaptic traction force is modeled via an uncapped actin-clutch law F_syn(t) = η_clutch · CA(t) · v_ARFS(t) · Φ(ADC(t)) coupled to Hertzian + cortical-shell indentation of the Nalm6 target cell (E_target = 450 ± 120 Pa, T_0 = 50 pN/µm, η_clutch = 17.1 ± 4.6 pN·s/µm³). Because 3D indentation depth δ_syn and retrograde flow v_ARFS alone leave η_clutch and E_target structurally collinear along η_clutch / E_target = 0.038 s/µm (kinematic-only Fisher Information condition number κ = 16291940.5), anchoring E_target with the external AFM/MP-TFM stiffness prior resolves the collinearity ridge (reducing κ to 2686.37). Calibrating the mechano-potentiated perforin pore vs. Ca²+/ESCRT-III membrane repair layer (k_ESCRT = 0.045 min&supminus;¹) strictly on Untreated High-Killing CAR-T (Groups 1–5, n = 167, observed kill 57.5% vs. predicted 57.8%) and evaluating out-of-sample with zero refitting predicts the Lck-inhibitor Dasatinib 50 nM collapse across Groups 8*, 9*, 10* (n = 89: observed 4.5% vs. physics 10.3%, MAE = 5.5%; old area-only proxy predicts 29.5%, MAE = 23.9%). On Untreated Low-Killing donor batches (Groups 6–10, n = 163, observed 27.0%), synapse geometry and kinematics alone predict 51.1%, showing that ~24% of the inter-donor gap comes from smaller contact areas and lower MTOC docking while the remainder reflects unmeasured donor-intrinsic granzyme/perforin load.

Part III: Multi-Well (6 Wells, N = 6,931 Tracks) Fragment-Aware Renewal Null & Non-Circular Tests

In 3D confocal movies of motile T cells, tracks are finite trajectory segments rather than full-movie biographies: in Well 4 (n = 1,171 tracks), the median track duration is 5.27 h of the 22.3 h movie, only 3.8% span ≥90% of the recording, and there are 3.11 tracks per physical T cell (across all 6 wells, N = 6,931 tracks, median duration spans 2.00-5.27 h, mean 3.88 h).

  • Why "69.6% Are Not Serial Killers" Is Explained by Track Fragmentation Alone: Under a homogeneous renewal null (σ = 0, identical T cells with no "sticker" or "corpse-trap" sub-class), full uncensored 22.3 h trajectories meeting the Super-Engager filter split 7.8% / 12.7% / 79.5% (0 / 1 / ≥2 kills). Cutting those exact same homogeneous trajectories into empirical fragment lengths splits them 41.2% / 30.6% / 28.2%, which is statistically indistinguishable from the observed Well 4 split of 37.0% / 32.6% / 30.4% (χ² = 3.62, p = 0.164).
  • Eliminating Circular Outcome-Conditioned Tests: Comparing groups defined by kill count (0, 1, 2, ≥3) on bout count or bout duration is circular because kill events require contact bouts. All outcome-conditioned Mann–Whitney p-values are removed and replaced by 3 non-circular tests replicated across n = 6 independent wells (DemoDataset1–6, N = 6,931 tracks):
    1. Test 1 (Unbroken Long-Contact Bouts ≥120 min with Zero Dead-Dye Increase): Across n = 6 wells, 49.6% ± 20.7% of unbroken bouts ≥120 min at the official 1.5 µm threshold (and 40.0% ± 17.2% at the expanded 5.0 µm threshold) produce zero local dead-dye increase (vs. 28.4% under the homogeneous renewal + step-recall null; well-level t-test p = 0.0536 at 1.5 µm, p = 0.158 at 5.0 µm).
    2. Test 2 (Crowding-Adjusted Inter-Kill Gap Correlation ρ(Δt_1, Δt_2)): Among tracks with ≥3 attributed kills (n = 95), the raw correlation between consecutive inter-kill intervals is ρ = +0.222 (p = 0.031), but after partialing out local organoid crowding and track duration it drops to ρ_adj = +0.049 (p = 0.635), matching the fragment-aware σ = 0 null with crowding (ρ_null = -0.037). Thus, consecutive inter-kill intervals provide no evidence for a large intrinsic rate variance (σ = 0.88 is not supported once crowding is controlled).
    3. Test 3 (Exposure-Matched Contact Topology Across n = 6 Wells): Across all 6 wells, mean correlation with organoid dead-dye exposure is ρ = +0.160 ± 0.099 for discrete contact-detach bouts vs. ρ = +0.093 ± 0.125 for cumulative contact duration (paired well-level p = 0.0777).
