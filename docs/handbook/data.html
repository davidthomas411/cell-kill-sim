Dataset Layers (lab/handbook/data.md)

All dataset layers in this project derive from the Fei lab's light-sheet single-cell CAR-T / Nalm6 cytotoxicity dataset deposited at ScienceDB (19235; Wang et al., Nat. Commun. 2024 / Sci. Data 2026) and tracked in lab/registry.csv.

1. Experimental Cohort Overview

  • 13 experiments (v01–v13) spanning three biological regimes:
  • Untreated high-killing CAR-T (5 donors, Groups 1–5): v01_6_40_120_0-6_hd1, v02_6_40_120_0-6_hd3, v03_6_40_120_0-6_hd4, v04_6_40_120_0-6_hd6, v05_6_40_120_0-6_hd7 (200 chambers total; 167 with trace_num == 1).
  • Untreated low-killing CAR-T (5 donors, Groups 6–10): v06_6_40_120_0-6_hd8, v07_6_39_120_0-6_hd9 (22 deposited ROIs), v08_6_40_120_0-6_hd2, v09_6_40_120_0-6_hd10, v10_6_40_120_0-6_hd5 (182 chambers total; 160 with trace_num == 1).
  • Dasatinib 50 nM CAR-T (3 paired donors hd2, hd10, hd5, Groups 8*–10*): v11_6_36_120_0-6_hd2_ds, v12_6_35_120_0-6_hd10ds, v13_6_40_120_0-6_hd5_ds (120 chambers total; 89 with trace_num == 1).
  • Temporal sampling: 502 total microgrid chambers (MIP_ROI_*), imaged for 20–22 visits at 10 min intervals (200–220 min total) with 5 actin sweeps per visit at 15 s intervals (10,510 complete chamber-visits; 52,545 complete sweeps; 63,053 total deposited sweep files including partial ROIs).

3. Layer Details

  1. lab_masks (*_predictions.h5): 3D uint8 array /predictions of shape (Z, 256, 256) (Z in [161, 195]) output by the Fei lab's 3D U-Net:
  2. 0: background
  3. 1: CAR-T cell interior / nucleus
  4. 2: Nalm6 target cell interior / nucleus
  5. 3: CAR-T cell membrane / cortex
  6. 4: Nalm6 target cell membrane / cortex
  7. raw_volumes (*.h5 and *_MIP*_405.tif):
  8. 3D raw .h5 files store /data of shape (3, Z, 256, 256) in uint16: Channel 0 = 405 nm SYTOX Blue dead-cell dye, Channel 1 = 488 nm Lifeact-eGFP F-actin, Channel 2 = 561 nm mApple-Mem target membrane.
  9. 2D MIPs (*_MIPxy_405.tif, *_MIPxz_405.tif) are exact uint16 projections (raw[0].max(axis=0).T and raw[0].max(axis=1).T) at sweep 1 of each visit (10,518 XY and 10,518 XZ images).
  10. step3_reproduction: Exact Python port of the Fei lab's instance segmentation (step2_instance_seg.m), contact surface extraction (cl_factor.m), T-cell trajectory tracker (synapse_tracking.m -> trace_num), and 2-chamber raw actin depletion / retrograde flow (ADC, ARFS). Passed Gate G0 external check against the published paper tables.
  11. step4_death_labels_v2: Per-visit and per-chamber target death calls using the contacted target instance (trace_num == 1 Track 1) and excluding the MIP_ROI_0 visit >= 19 saturation band. Blocked at Gate G2.
  12. step5_descriptors: 33 macro-scale (>= 1.4 um) 3D geometric descriptors of the contact patch, target convex-hull / reference-sphere cup indentation, and T-cell protrusion, computed at z = 0.1962 um and z = 0.2400 um. Blocked at Gates G1/G3.
  13. qc_flags & clean_subset_v0.1: Pre-outcome mask quality metrics (src/mask_qc.py) and the 1,213-visit candidate clean subset defined in clean-cell-rule@v1. Pending visual verification on gallery clean-2026-10-28.
