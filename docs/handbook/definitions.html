Definitions and Caveats (lab/handbook/definitions.md)

1. Voxel Size and Coordinate Conventions

  • Lateral voxel pitch (VOXEL_UM): 0.1962 um (src/constants.py), derived directly from the Fei lab's contact-area voxel conversion factor 0.0385 um^2 in external/feilab_CART_analysis/main/analysis/summary/summary.m (sqrt(0.0385) = 0.196214 um).
  • Optical lateral FWHM (OPTICAL_FWHM_UM): 0.32 um (320 nm isotropic Bessel light-sheet resolution reported in Wang et al. 2024).
  • Array axis ordering:
  • Deposited HDF5 files (*_predictions.h5 and raw <stem>.h5) store 3D volumes in (Z, Y, X) order (shape = (Z, 256, 256)).
  • MATLAB reads HDF5 arrays in transposed (X, Y, Z) order. Because bwdist (3D Euclidean distance transform) breaks equidistant Voronoi ties by linear scan-line index, exact numerical reproduction of the Fei lab's instance segmentation requires transposing (Z, Y, X) -> (X, Y, Z) via .transpose(2, 1, 0) prior to distance_transform_edt (edt_indices_matlab_xyz in src/reproduce_actin_measures.py).
  • Deposited 2D MIPs satisfy MIPxy_405 == raw[0].max(axis=0).T (shape (256, 256) in (X, Y)) and MIPxz_405 == raw[0].max(axis=1).T (shape (256, Z) in (X, Z)).

2. Axial Z-Spacing and U-Net Resolution Caveats

  1. Axial Z-spacing caveat (z = 0.1962 um vs z = 0.2400 um): Across uncontacted Nalm6 target cells, the 3D bounding box spans ~19% fewer Z slices than XY pixels (Delta Z / Delta XY = 0.807). This corresponds either to mild physical oblateness of cells resting on the microgrid chamber floor at nominal isotropic dz = 0.1962 um, or to an effective post-deskew axial step of ~0.24 um (0.1962 / 0.807 = 0.243 um). Every geometric descriptor in src/contact_descriptors.py is computed under both dz = 0.1962 um and dz = 0.2400 um (_z024 columns). Relative descriptors (cup_depth_over_radius, tcell_protrusion_frac, target_sphericity) vary by < 6% between the two axial pitches, whereas absolute volumes scale by 0.2400 / 0.1962 = 1.223.
  2. U-Net surface smoothing cutoff (>= 1.4 um wavelength): Comparison of 3D U-Net mask boundaries against raw 561-nm membrane stacks across 20 sweeps (.agy_step5res_report.md) established:
  3. Outward boundary bias: the U-Net target mask ((pred == 2) | (pred == 4)) sits +0.72 um (+3.69 voxels) outside the raw 561-nm half-maximum membrane contour.
  4. Spectral half-power (-3 dB, P_mask / P_raw = 0.50) cutoff wavelength: lambda_50% = 1.38 um on the synaptic contact patch (1.45 um away from contact). Below lambda = 1.15 um, > 60% of raw surface roughness power is erased by U-Net smoothing and voxel staircase discretization.
  5. Therefore, sub-micron microvillar fingers (0.1–0.5 um, de Jesus et al. 2024) cannot be measured from the deposited masks; all valid mask descriptors are restricted to macro-scale features (>= 1.4 um wavelength).

3. Contact Patch and Cup Depth Definitions (cup-measurement@v1)

  • Interior synaptic contact surface (s_real): In step2_instance_seg.m and cl_factor.m, the Voronoi interface between tracked T cell j and target (map_exp == 2 adjacent to map_exp == 3) has its peripheral radius-5 rim (imdilate(background, sphere(5))) overwritten as s_edge = -j. Only interior voxels (s_real = (surface == +j)) count toward contact area (CA_um2 = s_largest * 0.0385). Omitting the s_edge rim exclusion inflates CA_um2 by 25–30%.
  • Equivalent patch radius (a): patch_equiv_radius_um = sqrt(contact_area_um2 / pi).
  • Convex-hull cup depth (d = cup_depth_hull_p95_um): Given the 3D convex hull ConvexHull(pts_b_surf_um) of the solid contacted target cell and the unit synaptic normal n_syn pointing from target centroid to T-cell centroid, T-cell-facing hull facets are selected by (n_facet . n_syn) > 0.05. For each target boundary voxel on the contact patch (pts_c_um), d_hull_c is the inward perpendicular distance from the closest facing hull facet. cup_depth_hull_p95_um is the 95th percentile of d_hull_c across the patch.
  • Focusing ratio (cup_depth_over_radius): cup_depth_over_radius = cup_depth_hull_p95_um / max(0.20, patch_equiv_radius_um) (d / a).
  • T-cell protrusion fraction (tcell_protrusion_frac): Fraction of tracked T-cell voxels lying strictly inside the contacted target's 3D convex hull.
  • Simple Laplace push proxy (Route (b)): On a spherical target shell of radius R_target and cortical tension T_target indented to depth d over patch radius a, quasi-static Laplace normal-stress balance gives dimensionless push Pi_simple = p * R_target / T_target ≈ 2 + 4 * (R_target / a) * (d / a) and net indentation push Delta_Pi_indent = 4 * (R_target / a) * cup_depth_over_radius.

4. Target Death Rule (death-calling@v1)

  • 2D dead-dye mask (compute_2d_dead_mask in src/death_labels.py): 2D Gaussian filter (sigma = 3.0, truncate = 2.0), threshold > 404.0 ADU (calibrated to match the Fei lab's 3D > 398.0 ADU threshold after the +4.6 to +5.5 ADU 2D max-projection extreme-value shift), morphological closing with disk(3), and 8-connected area opening >= 20 px.
  • Per-visit positive call (dead_visit): Both overlap_xy_px >= 20 and overlap_xz_px >= 20 between the cleaned 2D dead-dye masks (d_xy, d_xz) and the 2D projections (b_xy, b_xz) of the 3D target instance contacted by the tracked T cell, after excluding MIP_ROI_0 visit >= 19 saturation-band frames.
  • Per-chamber death rules:
  • Any-Visit (ever_dead_any_visit_200m): >= 1 positive visit in visits 1–20 (matches summary.m C = max(B, [], 2)).
  • Sustained (ever_dead_200m): >= 2 consecutive positive visits in visits 1–20.

Channels (confirmed from the dataset paper, 2026-10-28)

Source: Sci Data dataset paper, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13009152/. - Channel 0, 405 nm: SYTOX Blue, the death dye (nuclei of dead cells). - Channel 1, 488 nm: Lifeact-EGFP, CAR-T cell actin. This makes the T cell visible. - Channel 2, 561 nm: mApple-Mem, the target (Nalm6) membrane. The paper also lists SiR-tubulin in this channel, for the 6th volume of each sequence only.

A membrane label explains the hollow target masks: a U-Net that fills the interior from a membrane signal fails when the shell is broken. An earlier note (notebook entry 2026-10-28-07) that channel 2 looked like a whole-cell label was wrong.

Z step conflict (open)

The folder names (e.g. 0-6) and the paper give an axial scanning step of 0.6 um. Measured cell shapes in the deposited volumes are nearly isotropic at dz = 0.196 um: targets span about 19% fewer slices than pixels. A raw 0.6 um z step would make cells look about 3x flattened, and they do not. The likely explanation is a skewed scan that is deskewed, giving an effective z of about 0.2-0.24 um, but the paper does not state this. Ask the Fei lab. Until then, absolute volumes and depths carry this uncertainty.
