Known Issues (lab/handbook/known-issues.md)

All issues below were identified by visual review of real-data gallery review-2026-10-28 (status/visual_review_2026-10-28.md) and technical audits. Every issue links to the notebook entry where it was recorded or addressed.

1. Cell Identity & Cup Measurement Issues (Gates G1 & G3)

  1. Fragmented target masks inflate 3D convex-hull cup depth
  2. Notebook entries: 2026-10-28-04-visual-review-2026-10-28, 2026-10-27-03-step5a-descriptors, 2026-10-28-05-clean-subset-rule
  3. Panels: cup_01 (10.15 um depth, target 2,821 um^3), cup_03 (9.18 um, 2,550 um^3), cup_09, cup_10 (5.10 um, depth/radius = 2.75), fit_unreach_01, deep_01.
  4. Mechanism & risk: When the target mask consists of multiple disconnected pieces, the 3D convex hull bridges across the pieces and cup_depth_hull_p95_um measures the inter-piece gap rather than a synaptic dent. Across 4,523 trace_num == 1 contact visits, median cup depth is 1.35 um when target_sphericity < 0.5 (n = 1,925) vs 0.72 um when target_sphericity >= 0.8 (n = 292; Spearman rho = -0.26). If dying targets fragment before SYTOX uptake, pre-death fragmentation could create part of the Step 5b / 7B Cox association (2026-10-27-04-step5b-result).
  5. Cells truncated by the field-of-view boundary (edge)
  6. Notebook entries: 2026-10-28-04-visual-review-2026-10-28, 2026-10-28-05-clean-subset-rule
  7. Panels: cup_05, cup_13.
  8. Mechanism: Cell bodies cut by the X/Y/Z crop border yield corrupted volumes, convex hulls, and indentation depths. Excluded by criterion 3 (touches_fov_edge == False) in clean-cell-rule@v1.
  9. Two target cells merged into one mask (merged)
  10. Notebook entries: 2026-10-28-04-visual-review-2026-10-28, 2026-10-28-05-clean-subset-rule
  11. Panels: dasa_vs_untr_untreated_02, deep_06, deep_07 (5.60 um depth), deep_08.
  12. Mechanism: Two adjacent round target cells fuse into a bilobed object with the T cell sitting in the cleft between them; the 3D hull bridges the cleft and records a spurious deep cup.
  13. Hollow ring-shaped or broken-shell target masks (hollow)
  14. Notebook entries: 2026-10-28-04-visual-review-2026-10-28, 2026-10-28-05-clean-subset-rule
  15. Panels: dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_11 (6.75 um depth), dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_14, dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_15, deep_vs_shallow_shallow_10, deep_vs_shallow_shallow_11.
  16. Mechanism: Target membrane class (pred == 4) forms an open ring or broken shell with missing interior (pred == 2), producing 5.8–6.8 um false cup depths.
  17. Severe experiment-specific mask degradation in donor hd10 and tiny debris fragments
  18. Notebook entries: 2026-10-28-04-visual-review-2026-10-28, 2026-10-28-03-step7-prereg-and-result, 2026-10-28-05-clean-subset-rule
  19. Panels: dasa_vs_untr_untreated_01, 03, 06, 07; dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, 11, 13 (154 um^3), 14, 15, 16 (37 um^3).
  20. Mechanism: Donor hd10 (v09 untreated clean fraction 0.05, v12 dasatinib clean fraction 0.16) is predominantly fragmented or hollow, which drove the hd10 donor outlier (cup_depth_over_radius = 0.50) and the single-donor dasatinib reduction in Step 7 (2026-10-28-03-step7-prereg-and-result).
