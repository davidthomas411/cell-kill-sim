Protocol clean-cell-rule@v1 · Date 2026-10-28 · draft

Protocol: Gate G1 Clean-Cell Subset Rule (clean-cell-rule-v1)

Purpose

Filter trace_num == 1 contact visits (n = 4,523 across 13 experiments) using strictly pre-outcome mask and geometry quality criteria (data/qc/per_visit_qc.csv, src/mask_qc.py) so that downstream cup-depth (G3) and killing-association (G4) analyses run only on visits where the tracked T cell and its contacted target are each one whole, separate, un-truncated cell.

No death labels (step4) or outcome associations (step5b, step7) were consulted when defining these criteria.

1. Inclusion Criteria (Per Contact Visit, trace_num == 1)

A contact visit is classified as clean (is_clean_visit == True) if and only if all five conditions hold simultaneously:

  1. Single connected extracted objects: target_n_components == 1 and tcell_n_components == 1 Both our extracted contacted target mask and our extracted tracked T-cell mask consist of a single 26-connected 3D component.
  2. Cohesive deposited U-Net target mask: target_raw_largest_frac >= 0.9 In the Fei lab's raw deposited U-Net predictions ((pred == 2) | (pred == 4) within the contacted target region), the largest connected component contains at least 90% of the target voxels. This rejects visits where the U-Net itself segmented the target into disconnected fragments.
  3. Not truncated by the field-of-view boundary: touches_fov_edge == False Neither the tracked T cell nor the contacted target touches the X, Y, or Z boundary of the 3D ROI crop.
  4. Plausible single-cell Nalm6 target volume: 200 <= target_volume_um3 <= 1500 Target volume at nominal voxel pitch (0.1962 um) lies between 200 um^3 (rejecting tiny subcellular debris fragments such as dasatinib_16 at 37 um^3 or dasatinib_13 at 154 um^3) and 1,500 um^3 (rejecting two-target merges such as cup_01 at 2,821 um^3 and cup_03 at 2,550 um^3).
  5. Chamber-level target volume stability: 0.7 <= target_vol_ratio_to_own_median <= 1.4 The target's volume at the current visit is within 0.7x to 1.4x of the median target volume across visits in the same chamber, rejecting sudden single-visit merges or splits with neighbouring cells.

2. Deliberately Excluded Criteria

  • Target solidity and sphericity (target_sphericity) are NOT used as filters. A genuine deep synaptic indentation lowers target solidity and sphericity (Spearman rho = -0.26 with cup_depth_hull_p95_um and -0.34 with cup_depth_over_radius). Filtering on sphericity or solidity would selectively discard real deep cups and bias the primary biological quantity under study.

3. Resulting Cohort Size (from Masks Only)

  • Overall: 1,213 of 4,523 trace_num == 1 contact visits (26.8%) pass all five criteria; 243 chambers have >= 1 clean contact visit and 111 chambers have >= 4 clean contact visits.
  • Clean fraction by experiment:
  • Untreated high-killing: v01 (hd1) 0.28, v02 (hd3) 0.34, v03 (hd4) 0.34, v04 (hd6) 0.40, v05 (hd7) 0.29
  • Untreated low-killing: v06 (hd8) 0.19, v07 (hd9) 0.16, v08 (hd2) 0.38, v09 (hd10) 0.05, v10 (hd5) 0.31
  • Dasatinib 50 nM: v11 (hd2_ds) 0.04, v12 (hd10ds) 0.16, v13 (hd5_ds) 0.14
  • Analytical scope: The clean subset provides sufficient sample size for within-experiment analyses in v01–v05, v08, and v10, but leaves donor hd10 (18 untreated clean visits) and pooled dasatinib (60 clean visits) too thin to support the 5-vs-5 donor comparison or the dasatinib control on their own without mask repair or re-segmentation from raw volumes.

4. Verification Requirement (G1)

This subset remains status: draft (unverified in lab/registry.csv) until the clean-2026-10-28 gallery (figures/review_clean/, containing stratified random clean visits, extreme clean visits, and 40 rejected visits across the 5 failure rules with raw 561-nm membrane images under every panel) is reviewed panel by panel under gallery-review@v1.

Protocol cup-measurement@v1 · Date 2026-10-28 · draft

Protocol: 3D Contact Patch and Cup Depth Measurement (cup-measurement-v1)

Purpose

Quantify macro-scale (>= 1.4 um spatial wavelength) 3D synaptic contact geometry and target cell indentation depth from segmented T-cell and target masks (*_predictions.h5), as currently implemented in src/contact_descriptors.py.

Status: draft (unverified; blocked at Gates G1 and G3 because 3D convex-hull cup depth is inflated when the target mask is fragmented, merged, hollow, or truncated by the FOV edge).

1. Resolution Cutoff & Axial Pitch Dual-Evaluation

  1. Spatial wavelength cutoff (>= 1.4 um): Spectral comparison against raw 561-nm membrane stacks (.agy_step5res_report.md) showed that the 3D U-Net masks attenuate > 50% of surface roughness power below lambda_50% = 1.38 um (synapse) / 1.45 um (non-contact) and dilate the target boundary outward by ~0.72 um. All geometric descriptors are restricted to whole-cell and macro-cup scales (>= 1.4 um wavelength; smallest trustworthy bump diameter >= 0.7–1.0 um laterally, >= 0.9–1.2 um axially). High-frequency residual variance below 1.4 um is recorded solely as a discretization noise check (rough_below_cutoff_noise_um2, rough_below_cutoff_frac).
  2. Dual axial voxel pitch (z = 0.1962 um vs z = 0.2400 um): Lateral voxel pitch is VOXEL_UM = 0.1962 um (sqrt(0.0385 um^2)). Because uncontacted targets span ~19% fewer Z slices than XY pixels (Delta Z / Delta XY = 0.807), every descriptor is evaluated at both nominal isotropic dz = 0.1962 um and alternative dz = 0.2400 um (_z024 columns). Descriptors whose median shifts by > 10% between the two axial pitches are flagged in data/step5/descriptor_dictionary.csv.

2. Cell & Contact Surface Extraction (process_one_chamber_descriptors)

  1. Instance segmentation: Runs instance_seg_matlab_xyz in MATLAB (X, Y, Z) dimension order (transpose(2, 1, 0)):
  2. T-cell instances from nucleus pred == 1 (bwareaopen >= 3000 voxels) and membrane pred == 3 (Voronoi assignment within 25-voxel sphere of nucleus, >= 1000 voxels, 26-connected hole fill).
  3. Target instances from nucleus pred == 2 and membrane pred == 4.
  4. Tracked T cell and contacted target body:
  5. Selects the tracked T-cell instance (tracked_tcell_id from src/reproduce_contact_area_tracked.py).
  6. Computes the Voronoi contact surface surface_mask = (map_exp == 2) & imdilate(map_exp == 3, sphere(1)) and overwrites the peripheral radius-5 rim (edge_mask = surface_mask & imdilate(map_exp == 1, sphere(5))) with -j, leaving interior synaptic contact voxels s_real = (surface == +j).
  7. Selects the largest 26-connected patch component s_largest_mask of s_real and identifies the contacted target body b_body (fill_solid_cell_mask: 26-connected target component containing the contacted target instance, 3D hole-filled plus 2D slice hole-filled along Z, Y, and X).
  8. Extracts target surface boundary voxels b_surf = b_body & ~binary_erosion(b_body, STRUCT6) and marks contact patch boundary voxels is_contact_on_b_surf.

3. Geometric & Indentation Descriptors (compute_descriptors_for_z_pitch)

Let pts_b_surf_um be the physical coordinates (z * dz, y * dxy, x * dxy) of all target boundary voxels and pts_c_um the physical coordinates of the synaptic contact patch voxels on the target surface:

  1. Contact patch size & orientation:
  2. contact_area_um2 = s_largest_vox * 0.0385 * area_stretch
  3. patch_equiv_radius_um = sqrt(contact_area_um2 / pi) (a)
  4. Unit synaptic normal n_syn: unit vector from target centroid b_center_um toward T-cell centroid t_center_um (representing the inward indentation direction into the target).
  5. Orthonormal tangent plane basis (e1, e2) perpendicular to n_syn; patch_elongation = sqrt(lambda_max / lambda_min) of the 2D covariance of pts_c_um in (e1, e2).
  6. 3D Convex-Hull Indentation (cup_depth_hull_p95_um):
  7. Computes the 3D convex hull hull_b = scipy.spatial.ConvexHull(pts_b_surf_um).
  8. Selects hull facets facing the T cell: facing = (eqs[:, :3] @ n_syn) > 0.05.
  9. For each contact patch point x in pts_c_um, computes the inward perpendicular distance to the closest facing hull plane: d_hull_c(x) = max(0, min_{f in facing} -(n_f . x + offset_f)).
  10. cup_depth_hull_p95_um: 95th percentile of d_hull_c across the contact patch (d).
  11. cup_depth_hull_max_um: maximum of d_hull_c.
  12. cup_volume_hull_um3: sum(d_hull_c) * (contact_area_um2 / N_c).
  13. cup_depth_over_radius: dimensionless focusing ratio cup_depth_hull_p95_um / max(0.20, patch_equiv_radius_um) (d / a).
  14. tcell_protrusion_vol_um3 and tcell_protrusion_frac: volume and fraction of tracked T-cell voxels lying strictly inside hull_b.
  15. Regularized Reference-Sphere Indentation (cup_depth_sphere_p95_um):
  16. Fits a sphere (c_sph, R_sph) to the non-contact target boundary points pts_nc_um regularized toward (b_center_um, r_eq_target).
  17. d_sph_pos(x) = max(0, R_sph - ||x - c_sph||), summarized by its 95th percentile cup_depth_sphere_p95_um and integrated volume cup_volume_sphere_um3.
  18. Per-Visit and Per-Chamber Aggregation:
  19. Per-visit descriptors average across contact sweeps (in_contact == True) within each visit (data/step5/per_visit_descriptors.csv).
  20. Pre-death chamber summaries (src/step5b_association_check.py) evaluate (A) the first contact visit strictly before death and (B) the mean across all contact visits strictly before death (visits 1–20).

4. Known Failure Modes Blocking Gates G1 & G3

Visual review (status/visual_review_2026-10-28.md) established that cup_depth_hull_p95_um measures false indentation whenever the target mask violates single-cell solidity: - Fragmented target masks (cup_01: 10.15 um, cup_03: 9.18 um, cup_10: 5.10 um): hull_b bridges across separate pieces and measures the inter-piece gap. - Two merged targets (deep_07: 5.60 um): hull_b bridges the groove between two distinct cells. - Hollow shell masks (dasatinib_11: 6.75 um): interior void inflates hull distance. - FOV edge truncation (cup_05, cup_13): flat image border distorts hull geometry. Must be restricted to clean-cell-rule@v1 verified visits before G3 signoff.

Protocol death-calling@v1 · Date 2026-10-28 · draft

Protocol: Target Cell Death Calling (death-calling-v1)

Purpose

Determine per-visit and per-chamber target cell death labels and death times across all 502 chambers (10,510 chamber-visits in 13 experiments) from deposited 2D 405-nm SYTOX Blue maximum-intensity projections (*_MIPxy_405.tif, *_MIPxz_405.tif) and 3D U-Net segmentation masks (*_predictions.h5), as currently implemented in src/death_labels.py (v2).

Status: draft (unverified; blocked at Gate G2 pending gallery/label mismatch resolution, 3D vs 2D neighbour-overlap verification, and threshold sensitivity analysis).

1. Reference 3D Rule (Fei Lab MATLAB Code)

In external/feilab_CART_analysis/main/step2_instance_seg.m and analysis/summary/dead_count.m:

  1. Raw 3D Channel 0 (405 nm SYTOX dead-cell dye, uint16) is smoothed with an anisotropic 3D Gaussian filter (sigma = [3, 3, 1] voxels in (Y, X, Z), kernel size [7, 7, 3], replicate padding) and thresholded at > 398.0 ADU.
  2. The binary mask is closed with a 6-connected radius-3 sphere (strel('sphere', 3)), area-opened to keep 26-connected components with >= 20 voxels (bwareaopen(..., 20, 26)), and dilated by radius 10 voxels (d_dil10_3d).
  3. A segmented target instance b_inst == i_id that touches the tracked T-cell synaptic contact surface (s_mask & sphere_dilate_edt(bi, 1.0)) is called dead at that sweep if any(bi & d_dil10_3d) is non-empty.
  4. Verified on all 20 local 3D raw volumes (data/step4/calibration_20_raw_volumes.csv): deposited 2D MIPs satisfy MIPxy_405 == raw[0].max(axis=0).T and MIPxz_405 == raw[0].max(axis=1).T with 0 ADU maximum difference.

2. Current 2D Projection Implementation (src/death_labels.py v2)

Because 3D raw .h5 volumes were initially available for only 20 sweeps whereas 2D 405-nm MIPs (*_MIPxy_405.tif, *_MIPxz_405.tif) are deposited for all 10,510 visits, src/death_labels.py implements a 2D projection rule:

Step 2.1: Contacted Target Instance Extraction (v2 update)

  • On sweeps 1..5 of each visit, 3D target instances are segmented via instance_seg_matlab_xyz(pred == 2, pred == 4) (bwareaopen(nucleus, 3000, 26) + Voronoi membrane assignment in MATLAB (X, Y, Z) order + 26-connected hole fill).
  • For the 416 chambers with a single tracked T cell (trace_num == 1 from src/reproduce_contact_area_tracked.py), only the target instance(s) contacted by Track 1 at that visit are retained. If no contact occurs on that visit, the chamber's primary contacted target instance (nearest centroid to the tracked target trajectory) is projected.
  • For the 86 chambers with trace_num != 1, the target instance contacted by any T cell is used and flagged (included_by_trace_rule = False).
  • The 3D contacted target mask is projected to match the MIP transpose convention:
  • b_xy = target_3d.max(axis=0).T
  • b_xz = target_3d.max(axis=1).T
  • (Legacy v1 columns *_all_targets_v1 projected all raw target voxels (pred == 2) | (pred == 4) without instance filtering).

Step 2.2: MIP_ROI_0 Late Stage-Origin Saturation Band Exclusion

  • In all 13 / 13 experiments, MIP_ROI_0 at visit >= 19 (t >= 190 min, 39 / 39 visits) contains a left-edge vertical 405-nm saturation band across columns 0..W-1 (W in [31, 121] px, column-0 median 548–915 ADU, peak 14,505–25,317 ADU).
  • Any visit satisfying (np.median(mip_xy[:, 0]) > 404.0) and (mip_xy.max() > 5000) is flagged as roi0_artifact_flag = True and excluded from triggering a positive death call (9 artifact-only false-positive chamber deaths removed).

Step 2.3: 2D Dead-Dye Mask (compute_2d_dead_mask)

  • Applied independently to mip_xy and mip_xz:
  • 2D Gaussian filter (scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter, sigma = 3.0, mode = 'nearest', truncate = 2.0).
  • Threshold strictly above THRESH_2D_ADU = 404.0 ADU (compensating for the +4.6 to +5.5 ADU extreme-value shift of taking the maximum over Z = 166–190 or Y = 256 slices relative to the 3D threshold 398.0 ADU).
  • 2D binary closing with a radius-3 disk (disk(3)).
  • 8-connected area opening keeping components with >= MIN_COMPONENT_AREA_2D = 20 pixels.

Step 2.4: Per-Visit and Per-Chamber Death Calls

  • Per-visit positive call (dead_visit): overlap_xy_px = sum(d_xy & b_xy) >= 20 AND overlap_xz_px = sum(d_xz & b_xz) >= 20 (and not roi0_artifact_flag).
  • Per-chamber definitions (computed over both the Full Recording of 20–22 visits and the Fixed 200-Minute Window of visits 1–20):
  • Any-Visit rule (ever_dead_any_visit_200m): >= 1 positive visit (matching summary.m C = max(B, [], 2)). death_visit is the first positive visit (death_time_min = death_visit * 10.0).
  • Sustained rule (ever_dead_200m): >= 2 consecutive positive visits. death_visit is the first visit of the first sustained run.

3. Open Issues Blocking Gate G2

  1. Gallery vs table mismatch (label_mismatch): In figures/review/death_labels/, panels rendered raw (pred == 2) | (pred == 4) outlines while the v2 table used contacted target instances, causing death_surviving_19, death_surviving_24, and death_killed_07 to disagree between panel overlay text and chamber label.
  2. Possible overlap discrepancy: death_dasatinib_30 visit 5 reports 27 px overlap while the dye contour appears outside the target outline in both views.
  3. 2D projection neighbour overlap (neighbour_overlap): A dye-positive bystander cell lying above/below or behind the contacted target can overlap in both XY and XZ projections (death_surviving_17, death_killed_05, death_killed_09).
  4. Narrow threshold margin: Background median is ~400 ADU vs threshold 404.0 ADU; borderline calls (death_killed_01 at 404.3 ADU) require threshold sensitivity curves across 403–410 ADU and 20–50 px.

Protocol gallery-review@v1 · Date 2026-10-28 · reviewed

Protocol: Gallery Review (gallery-review-v1)

Purpose

No dataset stage feeds the next until a real-image gallery for that stage has been inspected panel by panel and passes its stage gate (G1–G5). Matching summary numbers in a published table does not validate segmentation masks or extracted measurements.

1. Panel Selection (Stratified Random + Extremes + Borderlines)

Every stage-gate gallery renders real experimental data (*_predictions.h5, raw 561-nm membrane / 488-nm actin .h5 volumes fetched via src/download_raw.py --file-list, and 405-nm SYTOX MIPs) across three mandatory strata using a fixed random seed (20261028):

  1. Stratified random examples: sampled across all three biological conditions (Untreated high-killing CAR-T, Untreated low-killing CAR-T, Dasatinib 50 nM CAR-T) and across donors (hd1–hd10).
  2. Extremes of each measured quantity: maximum and minimum values (top 2–3 and bottom 2–3) of the stage's primary metrics (e.g., cup_depth_hull_p95_um, cup_depth_over_radius, target_volume_um3, target_raw_largest_frac, pi_solved, push_apart_um).
  3. Borderline threshold cases: visits or chambers closest to decision boundaries (e.g., within a few pixels of the 20-px 405-nm overlap cutoff, near the 404.0 ADU dye threshold, or closest to each of the five clean-cell QC thresholds).

2. Visual Standard Across All Panels

  1. Raw images underneath: Every 3D slice panel must show the raw fluorescence image (561-nm target membrane and/or 488-nm F-actin) beneath our extracted outlines, alongside the deposited U-Net labels (classes 0–4) when diagnosing segmentation failures.
  2. Fixed colour scheme:
  3. Tracked target outline: blue solid (#1F4E79)
  4. Tracked T-cell outline: orange solid (#C0561F)
  5. Other cells in field: grey fill/outline (explicitly labelled)
  6. Contact patch voxels: purple squares (#6B4C9A)
  7. Target convex hull / reference surface: green dashed (#2E7D5B)
  8. Measured cup depth vector / dead-dye contour: red (#B3261E)
  9. Solved model shapes: dashed outlines in fixed plot colours (#6B4C9A target, #111111 T cell)
  10. A complete legend/key and all numerical metrics from index.csv must appear on every panel.

3. Verdict Scale & Controlled Tag Vocabulary

Each reviewer inspects every panel at full resolution (via tools/review_app.py or contact sheets) and records a row in lab/reviews/<gallery_id>.csv with:

  • Verdict (verdict):
  • ok — Cell identity, segmentation contour, overlay alignment, and computed measurement are physically sound and match the underlying raw image.
  • problem — A segmentation error, truncation, merge, hollow shell, projection overlap artefact, label mismatch, or solver failure invalidates the measurement on this panel.
  • unsure — Ambiguous boundary, faint dye signal near threshold, or missing raw channel requiring secondary check.
  • Controlled tags (tags, semicolon-separated):
  • fragmented — Target or T cell is split into multiple disconnected pieces.
  • merged — Two distinct cells are fused into a single mask object.
  • hollow — Ring-shaped or broken-shell mask with unfilled interior.
  • edge — Cell body is truncated by the field-of-view border.
  • tiny — Implausibly small cell fragment (< 200 um^3).
  • misaligned — Reference or starting mesh does not align with the real cell.
  • drift — Solved mechanical mesh translates off the real cell mask (2–3 um rigid-body drift) or re-solve disagrees with continuation curve.
  • label_mismatch — Panel overlay numbers disagree with the chamber/visit table label.
  • neighbour_overlap — Dye-positive bystander cell overlaps target projection in XY/XZ without dye inside the contacted target.
  • real_death — Confirmed target death with SYTOX dye appearing inside the contacted target and persisting across visits.
  • clean — Whole, single, solid, well-segmented T cell and target away from FOV edges.
  • other — Issue not covered above (must be explained in note).

4. Independent Second Reviewer & Gate Signoff

  1. Primary complete pass (claude): Reviews 100% of panels in the gallery, logs per-panel findings, and summarizes failure rates by layer:
  2. (a) U-Net labels wrong while raw cell is whole (lab segmentation error),
  3. (b) U-Net labels sound but extracted object wrong (our extraction code bug),
  4. (c) Raw cell itself blebbing/fragmenting (real biology / possible death signal).
  5. Independent second reviewer (user / PI): Reviews panels independently in tools/review_app.py on key stage gates (G1, G2, G3). The review app and static site display all reviewers' verdicts side by side and highlight any panel where reviewers disagree.
  6. Iterate and lock: Code or subset rules are fixed to resolve identified problems, the same panels are re-rendered, and the gallery is re-reviewed until pass criteria hold and the notebook entry is signed off.
