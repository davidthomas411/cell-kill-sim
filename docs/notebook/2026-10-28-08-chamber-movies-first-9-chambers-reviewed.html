Question

Do the chamber movies (src/review/movies.py: raw top/side views of all visits, mask outlines, and a strip chart of cup depth, contact area, target volume and the death call) let us check the per-visit numbers against what the cells do? What do they show for the 9 fully downloaded chambers?

What was run

src/review/movies.py, run by agy (Argon High). It produced 18 clips (visits and burst) of 157-949 KB each, listed in figures/movies/index.csv.

What was looked at

The first, middle and last frame of every visits movie (9 chambers), reviewed by Claude.

Observations

  • The format works. Death-dye yellow, the T cell (green) and the target (magenta) are clearly separable, and the strip chart makes numeric jumps easy to match to images.
  • Possible missed death, v03 ROI 30 (labelled survived): at visit 20 a magenta cell is bright yellow with dye. Contact had ended (contact area 0), so either this is the contacted target dying after the T cell left, or it is a neighbour. Check identity across visits.
  • Death calls flicker, v03 ROI 6 (killed at visit 8): the per-visit call alternates dead and alive after visit 8, and visit 20 is "alive" while yellow dye is visible. Is a dead call lost when the dying target's mask fragments or the contact ends? Same pattern at v01 ROI 32, where only visits 9-11 are called dead although the target stays dye-positive.
  • Volume jumps from merges, v05 ROI 8: target volume spikes to 2,277 um3 at visit 10 (two cells merged into one mask), with a matching cup-depth spike.
  • v06 ROI 28 (low-killing donor hd8): fragmented, scattered outlines. Target volume is 17 um3 at visit 1, and the chamber is crowded with dying cells. Low-quality chamber.
  • Tracking switches target, v02 ROI 3: contact ends at visit 2, and the outlined "target" later is a different cell.

Decision

Movies become the standard review view. G2 (death labels) needs a check of the whole death timeline per chamber, not single visits, including dye on the contacted target after contact ends.

Open issues

  • Death labels: explain the dead/alive flicker in v03 ROI 6, and the missed late death in v03 ROI 30.
  • Raw volumes exist for only 9 chambers. Movies for more chambers need one sweep per visit downloaded (about 1.5 GB per chamber).
