Question

Is cup_depth_hull_p95_um (src/contact_descriptors.py:709-715) biased? In particular, does restricting the distance to T-cell-facing hull facets inflate depth (suspected from qc_clean_02, a flat-looking contact recorded at 2.87 um)?

What was run

python3 src/validate_cup_depth.py      # synthetic cases a-f + real-panel re-measure
python3 -m unittest tests.test_cup_depth

Run by agy (Gemini 4 Argon High); report .agy_cuptest_report.md.

What was looked at

  • figures/cup_depth_validation.png (summary)
  • figures/review/cup_validation/: b_dent_R5_a3_d2, case_f_hollow_shell, f_hollow_R5_d1p0_filled_mask (reviewed by Claude).

Observations

  • No facing-facet bias. On a flat cut (7 cases, expected 0) the result is 0.000. A spherical dent of 2 um measures 1.96 um; a 3 um dent measures 2.75-2.94 um; a tilted synapse axis (10-30 deg) gives no change; a wide bowl with 0.3 um sag measures 0.196 um (one voxel). All-facets distance matches the recorded value in 17/22 real panels. My concern was wrong.
  • Merged targets inflate depth. Two spheres with the T cell between them (expected cleft 0) give 2.3-4.0 um. This matches gallery panels deep_07, qc_edge_01, qc_edge_02 and dasa_vs_untr_untreated_02.
  • Visit means are contaminated by single bad sweeps. qc_clean_02 visit 2: the displayed sweep measures 0.71 um; the visit mean of 2.87 um includes sweeps 3-4 where a second target merged (2,450 um^3; 5.6-5.8 um "cups"). Per-visit descriptors average 5 sweeps, so QC must run per sweep.
  • The sphere-reference depth (cup_depth_sphere_p95_um) is badly biased: 0.3-3.0 um on flat contacts. It should not be used.
  • Hollow-shell case f: the shell without filling measures 0.98 um (expected 1.0). After fill_solid_cell_mask it measures 0.196 um, and the figure shows the cup filled in. How the synthetic shell was built is not clear, so this is an open question, not a finding. Solid dented cells (case b) are measured correctly.

Decision

Keep the hull-depth definition. G3 work: QC per sweep (not per visit), exclude merged-target sweeps, drop the sphere-reference depth. Recompute visit descriptors from clean sweeps only.

Open issues

  • Confirm whether fill_solid_cell_mask can erase real cups on real hollow masks (compare filled vs unfilled depth on the hollow panels dasatinib_09, 11, 14).
  • RESOLVED (handbook definitions.md): channel 2 is mApple-Mem membrane (561 nm), as the captions say. Earlier reading: its signal is brighter in the target core than at the rim (e.g. v01 ROI_2 stem 00495: core 560, rim 490, background 401), so it is likely a whole-cell label. Channel 1 marks the T cell, channel 0 the death dye. Correct the captions.
