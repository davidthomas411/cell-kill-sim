Question

Under the pre-registered analysis plan (status/step5b_preregistration.md, committed in b3be277 before comparing any descriptor against outcomes), do pre-death contact shape and indentation descriptors associate with target cell death within experiments, hold out across experiments, separate high- vs low-killing donors, or respond to the drug control?

Observations

  1. Test 1 — Within-experiment Cox model (UNVERIFIED, n = 365–366 pre-death trace_num == 1 chambers):
  2. On unverified descriptors, 7 / 12 primary descriptors had BH q < 0.05 in both Summary A (first contact visit strictly before death) and Summary B (mean over pre-death contact visits in visits 1–20):
    • cup_depth_over_radius: Summary A unadjusted HR = 1.420 per SD ([1.171, 1.721], q = 0.0034, same direction in 3/3 condition blocks; CA-adjusted HR = 1.375, q = 0.0200); Summary B unadjusted HR = 1.547 ([1.268, 1.889], q = 2.09e-4).
    • cup_depth_hull_p95_um: Summary A unadjusted HR = 1.388 (q = 0.0034, CA-adjusted HR = 1.505, q = 0.0200); Summary B unadjusted HR = 1.621 (q = 3.22e-5).
    • tcell_protrusion_frac: Summary A unadjusted HR = 1.382 (q = 0.0035, CA-adjusted HR = 1.547, q = 0.0200).
    • target_sphericity: Summary A HR = 0.765 (q = 0.0129); Summary B HR = 0.654 (q = 1.18e-4).
  3. Test 2 — Leave-one-experiment-out logistic AUC (UNVERIFIED): Adding the primary descriptor family to contact area + condition did not improve held-out AUC (Delta AUC = -0.025 in Summary A, -0.009 in Summary B).
  4. Test 3 — Donor separation (n = 5 high vs 5 low untreated donors, UNVERIFIED): No descriptor separated high- vs low-killing donor medians (min Mann-Whitney p = 0.151).
  5. Test 4 — Drug control (hd2, hd10, hd5, UNVERIFIED): In Summary B, dasatinib lowered cup_depth_hull_p95_um and tcell_protrusion_frac in 3/3 paired donors (0/3 and 2/3 at first contact in Summary A).
  6. Confounding risk identified in visual review (status/visual_review_2026-10-28.md): Target fragmentation lowers target_sphericity and artificially inflates cup_depth_hull_p95_um via 3D hull bridging (deep_01, deep_06, deep_07, deep_08). If dying targets begin to bleb or fragment before 405-nm SYTOX dye crosses the threshold, pre-death fragmentation could generate part or all of the Test 1 association (target_sphericity itself had HR = 0.654).

Decision

Result is UNVERIFIED (status: unverified in lab/registry.csv) and placed on hold at Gate G4. The pre-registered analysis must be rerun on G1–G3 verified clean cells and verified death labels, keeping these earlier numbers solely for before/after audit comparison.
