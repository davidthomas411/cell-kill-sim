Observations

  1. Cup measurement & mask defects (invalidates unverified step5_descriptors, step5b_associations, step7_focused_pushing):
  2. Fragmented targets: Target mask is split into separate pieces (cup_01 10.2 um, cup_03 9.2 um, cup_09, cup_10 5.1 um, fit_unreach_01, deep_01), so the 3D convex hull bridges the inter-piece gap. Across 4,523 contact visits, median cup depth is 1.35 um (sphericity < 0.5, n = 1,925) vs 0.72 um (sphericity >= 0.8, n = 292; Spearman rho = -0.26).
  3. FOV edge truncation: cup_05, cup_13.
  4. Two merged targets: dasa_vs_untr_untreated_02, deep_06, deep_07 (5.6 um), deep_08.
  5. Hollow target shells: dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, 11, 14, 15, deep_vs_shallow_shallow_10, 11 (5.8–6.8 um false cups).
  6. Donor hd10 (v09, v12): Mostly fragmented or hollow (dasa_vs_untr_untreated_01, 03, 06, 07; dasa_vs_untr_dasatinib_09, 11, 14, 15) plus tiny fragments (dasatinib_16: 37 um^3, dasatinib_13: 154 um^3).
  7. Clean cells: Whole, separate cells measure sensibly and match raw membrane contours (cup_14–16, shallow_12, 13, deep_04, dasa_vs_untr_untreated_04, 05, 08).
  8. Mechanical model defects (invalidates step6b_fits):
  9. Solved target drifts 2–3 um off the mask (fit_reach_03, 04, 05; fit_unreach_10, 12).
  10. Re-solving at fitted Pi disagrees with the continuation curve in 9 / 32 fit panels (fit_reach_01, 03, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 13; fit_unreach_09).
  11. Reference target misaligned (fit_unreach_03, 10, 12, 13, 15), starting overlaps up to 11.0 um (overlap_01, 03, 12), and curve inversion mismatch in fit_reach_14.
  12. Death label defects (blocks step4_death_labels_v2):
  13. Narrow signal margin (404 ADU threshold vs ~400 ADU background; death_killed_01 at 404.3 ADU).
  14. Overlap bug check needed on death_dasatinib_30 v5 (27 px reported outside outline).
  15. Gallery/table mismatch on death_surviving_19, death_surviving_24, and death_killed_07.
  16. Neighbour projection overlap on death_surviving_17, death_killed_05, death_killed_09.
